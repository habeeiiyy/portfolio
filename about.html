Curious & Self-Driven Backend Developer

Habeeb P

I'm a backend-focused developer from Palakkad, Kerala, having completed my Plus Two Higher Secondary Education at GVHSS Alanallur. My journey into tech started with a curiosity about how data moves behind the scenes — from a simple Python script to relational databases, REST APIs, and asynchronous queues.

When I'm not writing Django models or debugging React state, you’ll usually find me playing chess, exploring Linux environments, or digging into how open-source projects organize their code. I view software engineering the same way I view chess: strategic foresight, recognizing patterns, and finding the cleanest move under pressure.

I don't pretend to be an enterprise veteran — I am an intensive, self-learning and curious developer engaged in deep technical research, building real projects like SmashUp to test my limits and continuously sharpen my problem-solving skills.
